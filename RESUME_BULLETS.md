# Evidence-based project summary

- Built a reproducible Python CLI that fetches and verifies an attributed World Bank WDI snapshot,
  validates its provenance, and generates offline descriptive reports.
- Precommitted the analysis plan; analyzed 209 complete 2022 country/economy observations and
  reported a Spearman rank association with a predefined largest-population sensitivity check.

The reported association is descriptive, not causal. Dataset indicators are in current US dollars
and population counts; see `ANALYSIS_PLAN.md` and `DATASET.md` for limits and attribution.
